# INSIDE THE TANK – Startup Investment Analysis Case Study

## Project Overview

**Inside the Tank** is an educational, case-based financial analysis project designed to help students understand how investors evaluate startups before making investment decisions.

Inspired by the investment evaluation process featured on *Shark Tank*, this project uses a fictional startup, **FreshBite Foods Pvt. Ltd.**, to demonstrate how financial performance, profitability, unit economics, customer metrics, cash flow, business valuation, and investment risks influence funding decisions.

The project combines practical Excel-based financial analysis with business case learning and a simulated investor negotiation exercise.

## Business Case: FreshBite Foods Pvt. Ltd.

FreshBite Foods Pvt. Ltd. is a fictional food and beverage startup specialising in healthy protein snacks. The company sells its products through online platforms, distributors, and retail stores.

After three years of operations, the founders approach potential investors seeking:

- **Investment Required:** ₹50 Lakhs
- **Equity Offered:** 5%
- **Implied Post-Money Valuation:** ₹10 Crore
- **Implied Pre-Money Valuation:** ₹9.5 Crore

Students take the role of investors and financial analysts to evaluate whether the proposed investment is financially attractive and commercially viable.

## Project Objectives

- Understand how investors evaluate startup investment proposals.
- Analyse financial statements and business performance.
- Evaluate revenue growth, profitability, and operating efficiency.
- Calculate unit economics and break-even points.
- Assess customer acquisition costs and customer lifetime value.
- Understand startup valuation and equity dilution.
- Identify financial and operational risks.
- Develop evidence-based investment recommendations.

## Financial Analysis Modules

The Excel workbook is structured around seven major analytical models:

| No. | Analysis Model | Key Areas |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Revenue & Business Growth | Revenue trends, YoY growth, sales volume, and sales channels |
| 02 | Profitability Analysis | Gross profit, EBITDA, net profit, and profit margins |
| 03 | Unit Economics | Contribution margin, variable costs, and break-even analysis |
| 04 | Customer & Marketing Analysis | CAC, repeat purchase rate, AOV, LTV, and LTV:CAC |
| 05 | Cash Flow & Financial Position | Cash flow, working capital, liquidity, and debt |
| 06 | Business Valuation & Investment | Pre-money valuation, post-money valuation, equity dilution, and investment scenarios |
| 07 | Investment Evaluation & Risk | Weighted evaluation, risk assessment, and investment recommendations |

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel:** Financial modelling, calculations, data analysis, and investment evaluation.
- **Financial Modelling:** Revenue forecasting, profitability analysis, unit economics, and valuation.
- **Statistical Analysis:** Growth rates, averages, standard deviation, correlation, and sensitivity analysis.
- **Business Analysis:** Risk assessment, scenario evaluation, and investment decision-making.
- **Microsoft Word:** Educational case study, learning materials, and instructor documentation.
