# Project Overview
## INSIDE THE TANK – Startup Investment Analysis Case Study

### 1. Introduction

Inside the Tank is an educational financial analysis and startup investment case study inspired by the investment evaluation process featured on *Shark Tank*.

The project is designed to provide students with practical exposure to how investors evaluate startups before making funding decisions. It combines financial analysis, business evaluation, valuation techniques, risk assessment, and investment decision-making through a realistic fictional business scenario.

The project uses Microsoft Excel as the primary analytical tool and supporting educational documents to explain the concepts, calculations, and investment evaluation process.

### 2. Business Case

The case study revolves around **FreshBite Foods Pvt. Ltd.**, a fictional food and beverage startup specialising in healthy protein snacks.

The company has been operating for three years and sells its products through online platforms, distributors, and retail stores.

The founders approach potential investors with the following proposal:

| Investment Particulars | Details |
|---|---:|
| Investment Required | ₹50 Lakhs |
| Equity Offered | 5% |
| Implied Post-Money Valuation | ₹10 Crore |
| Implied Pre-Money Valuation | ₹9.5 Crore |
| Industry | Food & Beverage |

Students are required to examine the company's financial and operational performance, assess its investment potential, and decide whether the proposed deal is worth accepting, negotiating, or rejecting.
